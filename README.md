# Ali Hamza

Plant geneticist working at the interface of molecular biology and bioinformatics, with an
MSc (Hons) in Plant Breeding and Genetics from the University of Agriculture, Faisalabad.

I study how crop plants respond to disease and drought, using comparative genomics, transcriptomics
and reproducible analysis in R and Python.

**Interests:** plant stress genomics · plant–microbe interactions · gene family evolution · transcriptomics

### Selected work

- **WRKY transcription factors in Fabeae legumes**: comparative genomics and re-analysis of public RNA-seq
  under disease and drought in faba bean, pea, lentil and grass pea (manuscript in preparation)
- **Dof transcription factors in Fabeae legumes**: genome-wide identification, phylogenetics, synteny and
  expression (manuscript under revision, *BMC Plant Biology*)
- **[Tomato leaf disease classification](https://github.com/alihamza-bio/tomato-leaf-disease-cnn)**: transfer learning with MobileNetV2 on six leaf classes (Python, TensorFlow/Keras)

### Tools

R (DESeq2, Bioconductor, tidyverse, ggplot2) · Python (pandas, scikit-learn, TensorFlow/Keras) · Bash/Linux ·
RNA-seq (FastQC, HISAT2, featureCounts, kallisto) · phylogenetics (IQ-TREE) · reproducible workflows (targets, renv, git)
